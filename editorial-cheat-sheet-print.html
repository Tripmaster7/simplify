Editorial Cheat Sheet

WordPress Article Import Wizard | Keep this page printed near your workstation.

1) Quick Workflow

  1. Prepare article in Word and save as DOCX.
  2. Log in: the wizard opens directly (otherwise menu Article Import).
  3. Enter name or membership number of the real author.
  4. Upload DOCX, headline image, inline images; check categories.
  5. Click Generate Preview.
  6. Check warnings for author, links and images.
  7. Check draft or publish, then Complete Import.

2) Golden Rules

  • Use DOCX only (not DOC).
  • Give the author by full name or membership number.
  • No matching author: the default author is used and the preview warns. Correct the entry.
  • Editor account is internal only.
  • Update = upload again with same title and author. Only upload changed pictures.

3) Metadata Tags in DOCX

  • [TITLE: Your headline]
  • [SUBTITLE: Your sub-headline]
  • [HEADER 3 Headline 3] (body H3 block)
  • [HEADER 4 Headline 4] (body H4 block)
  • [WRITING_DATE: 2026-08-04] (post date; 04.08.2026 also works)
  • [AUTHOR_MEMBERSHIP: 01234]
  • [BIO: Short author bio] (optional; may span several paragraphs, end with ])
  • [RESTRICT] (start of members-only part; default: after 2nd paragraph, or 1st if 1,000+ characters)
  • [RESTRICT_START] = [RESTRICT]; [RESTRICT_END] is ignored (legacy)
  • H1 and H2 are reserved for title/subtitle only.
  • Use HEADER 3 and HEADER 4 tags inside article body.
  • The members-only part always ends before the author bio; the bio is public.

4) Inline Image Markers

  • [Bild 1]
  • [Bild 2: Caption]
  • [Image 3: English caption]
  • Numbering starts at 1.
  • Upload inline images in matching order.
  • If mismatch: preview shows warning.
  • Word formatting kept: bold, italic, links, bullet and numbered lists.
  • Not kept: underline, colours, fonts, tables. No Markdown.

5) Copy/Paste DOCX Template

[TITLE: ] [SUBTITLE: ] [WRITING_DATE: ] [AUTHOR_MEMBERSHIP: ] First paragraph... Second paragraph with [Bild 1: Caption]. [RESTRICT] (optional) Third paragraph with [Bild 2]. [HEADER 3 Background] Section paragraph... [HEADER 4 Detail] Detail paragraph... [BIO: ]

6) Final Checklist

  • DOCX format confirmed.
  • Author confirmed, no author warning.
  • Categories checked.
  • Tags are complete and valid.
  • Image markers and uploads match.
  • Preview checked; draft or publish chosen on purpose.
  • If shown: CHECK LINK must be fixed before publish.

Tip: print at 100% scale on A4 portrait. Keep this as your standard article submission format.
