Editorial Cheat Sheet

WordPress Article Import Wizard | Keep this page printed near your workstation.

1) Quick Workflow

  1. Prepare article in Word and save as DOCX.
  2. Open WordPress > Article Import Wizard.
  3. Enter membership number of the real author.
  4. Upload DOCX, headline image, inline images.
  5. Click Generate Preview.
  6. Check warnings for links and missing images.
  7. Click Create Draft From Preview.

2) Golden Rules

  • Use DOCX only (not DOC).
  • Membership number must be correct.
  • Editor account is internal only.
  • Public author comes from membership number.

3) Metadata Tags in DOCX

  • [TITLE: Your headline]
  • [SUBTITLE: Your sub-headline]
  • [WRITING_DATE: 2026-08-04]
  • [AUTHOR_MEMBERSHIP: 123456]
  • [AUTHOR_NAME: First Last] (optional)
  • [BIO: Short author bio] (optional)
  • [RESTRICT_START] (optional)
  • [RESTRICT_END] (optional)

4) Inline Image Markers

  • [Bild 1]
  • [Bild 2: Caption]
  • [Image 3: English caption]
  • Numbering starts at 1.
  • Upload inline images in matching order.
  • If mismatch: preview shows warning.

5) Copy/Paste DOCX Template

[TITLE: ] [SUBTITLE: ] [WRITING_DATE: ] [AUTHOR_MEMBERSHIP: ] [AUTHOR_NAME: ] [RESTRICT_START] First paragraph... Second paragraph with [Bild 1: Caption]. Third paragraph with [Bild 2]. [RESTRICT_END] [BIO: ]

6) Final Checklist

  • DOCX format confirmed.
  • Membership number confirmed.
  • Tags are complete and valid.
  • Image markers and uploads match.
  • Preview checked before draft creation.
  • If shown: CHECK LINK must be fixed before publish.

Tip: print at 100% scale on A4 portrait. Keep this as your standard article submission format.
